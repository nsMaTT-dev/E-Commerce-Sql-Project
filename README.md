# E-Commerce SQL & Power BI Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a fictional e-commerce business using SQL and Power BI, focusing on sales performance, product profitability, customer behavior, and store performance.

The project combines SQL-based data analysis with interactive Power BI dashboards to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that support data-driven decision-making.

The analysis covers multiple years of sales data and explores different aspects of the business, including revenue generation, profitability, customer segmentation, product performance, and store comparisons.

---

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze annual and monthly sales performance.
- Identify the products and categories generating the highest revenue and profit.
- Evaluate customer purchasing behavior and revenue contribution.
- Analyze sales performance across different stores.
- Compare store profitability and profit margins.
- Identify sales patterns across different days of the week.
- Develop interactive dashboards to communicate business insights effectively.

---

## Business Questions

The following business questions guided the SQL analysis and the development of the Power BI dashboards, covering the main aspects of the e-commerce business.

### 1. Annual Sales Performance
How has the business performed annually in terms of order volume, units sold, revenue, costs, and profit?

### 2. Monthly Sales Performance
How does sales performance vary from month to month in terms of order volume, revenue, and profit? Are there any significant trends or unusual fluctuations?

### 3. Product Revenue and Profitability
Which products generate the highest revenue and profit, and how do their sales volumes and costs compare?

### 4. Customer Purchase Performance
Which customers have placed at least five orders, and how do they compare in terms of purchase volume, total revenue, and average order value?

### 5. Customer Revenue by Income Range
How is revenue distributed across customer income groups (Low, Middle, and High Income), and which group contributes the most to total revenue and average revenue per customer?

### 6. Store Sales Performance
How do stores compare in terms of order volume, units sold, revenue, costs, and total profit?

### 7. Sales Metrics by Category
Which product categories generate the highest revenue, and how do they compare in terms of order volume, units sold, and average order value?

### 8. Store Profitability
Which stores achieve the highest profit margins, and how do their revenue, costs, and total profit compare?

### 9. Customer Purchase Frequency by Store
Which customers have placed at least four orders at the same store, and how much revenue and sales volume do they generate at each location?

### 10. Sales Performance by Weekday
Which days of the week generate the strongest sales results in terms of order volume, units sold, revenue, and profit?

## Key Business Insights

### Sales Performance
The annual analysis revealed differences in sales performance across the available years, with 2026 recording the highest annual revenue.

The monthly analysis also identified an unusually high revenue peak in October 2026, when revenue reached approximately **R$1.35 million**, significantly exceeding the figures recorded in previous months.

This concentration may be related to the synthetic data generation process and should be considered when interpreting the results.

### Product Performance
The product analysis highlighted differences in revenue generation and sales volume across products and categories.

Peripherals stood out in terms of units sold, while Notebooks generated the highest revenue among product categories.

### Customer Analysis
Customer segmentation revealed that the Middle Income group represented the largest customer segment.

The analysis also provided insights into customer revenue contribution, purchase frequency, and average order value, allowing the identification of high-value customers and purchasing patterns.

### Store Performance
The store analysis highlighted differences in revenue, profit, and profit margins across the five stores.

These metrics provide a comparative overview of store performance and profitability, helping identify the strongest-performing locations.
