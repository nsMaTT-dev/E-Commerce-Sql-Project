# Conclusion

This project explored the performance of a fictional e-commerce business through SQL queries and interactive Power BI dashboards, covering four key analytical areas: Sales, Products, Customers, and Stores.

The analysis provided insights into revenue generation, profitability, purchasing behavior, product performance, customer segmentation, and store comparisons.

The monthly revenue analysis also revealed an unusually high peak in October 2026, when revenue reached approximately R$1.35 million, significantly exceeding the figures recorded in previous months. This concentration may be related to the synthetic data generation process and should be considered when interpreting the results.

Among the key findings, 2026 recorded the highest annual revenue despite having fewer orders than 2024. The product analysis revealed that Peripherals led in units sold, while Notebooks generated the highest revenue among product categories. Customer segmentation showed that Middle Income customers represented the largest customer group, while the store analysis highlighted differences in revenue, profit, and profit margins across locations.

The project also involved data preparation, data quality investigation, SQL-based analysis, DAX calculations, and the development of interactive dashboards in Power BI.

Overall, this project demonstrates the application of data analysis techniques to transform raw business data into meaningful insights, supporting performance evaluation and data-driven decision-making.
